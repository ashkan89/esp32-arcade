# Arcade research and original implementations

Research checked publisher/platform pages on 2026-10-10. These are classic inspirations,
not licensed ports: new names, rules, code, procedural graphics and bosses are original.
No ROM, commercial source dump, sprite, soundtrack or executable was incorporated.
The interpretation column describes our design for this ESP32, portrait display and switches.
